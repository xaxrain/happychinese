HC 2-3 我的一天

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

  • Talk about their daily routine using simple Chinese sentences.
  • Tell the time and describe when they do different activities.
  • Use common vocabulary related to daily activities, such as 起床、吃饭、上学、上课、回家、做作业、睡觉.
  • Use the sentence pattern “我 + time + activity” to describe their day.
  • Use 早上、上午、中午、下午、晚上 to organise activities by time of day.
  • Ask and answer questions such as “你几点起床？” and “你几点上学？”.
  • Sequence daily activities and give a simple description of “我的一天” in Chinese.
  • Develop confidence in speaking about their own daily life using familiar Chinese vocabulary and sentence patterns.
