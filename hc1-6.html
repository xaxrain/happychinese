HC 1-6 我家不大

🎯 Learning Objectives
  • describe their home and family using simple Chinese sentences.
  • ask and answer questions about the size of a home, such as “你家大吗？” and “你家大不大？”
  • use basic adjectives to describe a home, such as 大 (big), 小 (small), 漂亮 (beautiful), and 不大 (not big).
  • use “很” and “不” to describe people and things, e.g. “我家很大。” / “我家不大。”
  • identify and use key vocabulary related to the home and family in simple conversations.
  • understand and respond to simple questions about where they live and what their home is like.
  • give a short spoken description of their own home using the sentence patterns learned in the lesson.
